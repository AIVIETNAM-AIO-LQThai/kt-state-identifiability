| ID | Decision |
|---|---|
| X6-D01 | **Question (chosen by the owner: "Adjust for feedback").** Following X5-D09: does a fitted model that contains outcome feedback recover sigma2_F* when F and feedback coexist, stop declaring F when only feedback is present, and keep the F test calibrated and powerful without feedback? Cells E1-E5 of Experiment 5 (E6 dropped), N {300, 1000}, lambda == 1 in the data, 2PL fits. Hypotheses H6a-H6e are provisional (protocol section 2) and are finalised at the Opus Stage-0 and pilot reviews. |
| X6-D02 | **Feedback-aware pairwise model, approximation L0** (protocol 1.3). Models B1-FB and B2-FB are the 2PL models plus kappa in [-1, 1] and tau_D in [0.5, 50]. Mean-field moments come from a forward recursion (m_t, v_t, c_st from e_r = 1 - Phi(a_r)). The direct pair effect is exact: cells with y_s = 0 use the shifted mean a_t^(0), and the conditional variance removes the direct term's share. Cell probabilities are P11 = Phi2(a_s, a_t^(1); rho), P10 = Phi(a_s) - P11, P01 = Phi(a_t^(0)) - Phi2(a_s, a_t^(0); rho), P00 = 1 - Phi(a_s) - P01. The model is the 2PL objective exactly at kappa = 0. Ignored: indirect paths, the error-F correlation, and the non-Gaussian remainder. **Fallback L1** (probit linearisation of earlier errors, which gives a joint latent covariance with the F x feedback cross term) is built only after an Opus review if G0-3 fails; simulated or indirect inference comes after that. tau_D is unidentified at kappa = 0; this is handled by multiple starts and reported. |
| X6-D03 | **Schedule-term comparison route (B2+eta).** The Experiment-5 covariate x is schedule-based, built from item-level expected error rates rather than the learner's own outcomes. A fitted eta x term can therefore remove only the marginal-mean footprint of feedback, not the within-session covariance that F absorbs. Prediction: sigma2_F* stays well above 0 in E3 and above 0.16 in E5 under B2+eta. This is tested at population level (G0-2, tau_x = 2) as a cheap check on the reasoning. |
| X6-D04 | **Stage-0 gates.** **G0-1, implementation:** kappa = 0 equals the 2PL objective and gradient to 1e-12; gradient against finite differences to relative 1e-6; cells sum to 1; L0 cell probabilities against empirical pair frequencies at N = 200,000 at the true E3 and E5 parameters; full suite passes (X3-F06 known). **G0-2, B2+eta (descriptive):** if E3 sigma2_F* <= 0.02 and E5 is within 0.03 of 0.16, the design is reconsidered. **G0-3, FB at population level:** E3 sigma2_F* <= 0.02 with expected T_FB at N = 1000 below 5.16; E5 abs(sigma2_F* - 0.16) <= 0.03; E1 abs(sigma2_F* - 0.16) <= 0.01 and abs(kappa*) <= 0.02; kappa* within +-20 % of the truth in E3 and E5 (E4 reported). Failure means STOP for Opus. **G0-4, identifiability and sizes:** Godambe SEs of sigma2_F*, kappa and tau_D; corr(sigma2_F-hat, kappa-hat); predicted power of T_FB in E1 and E5; expected T_FB in E2-E4; FB/2PL runtime ratio. |
| X6-D05 | **Code, branch, seeds.** New package `feedback_model/` (imports kt_trial, difficulty_free, discrimination_free and state_dependence; modifies none). Branch `exp/feedback-adjusted-F`, created from the HEAD of `exp/outcome-driven-dynamics` after the Experiment-5 closure commit. Seeds: Stage 0 20266001, pilot 20266002, confirmatory 20266101. The T_FB null reference is a warp bootstrap from the fitted B1-FB, simulated with feedback at (kappa-hat, tau_D-hat). Rough total of 150-250 CPU-h; the owner sets the cap at the pilot review. |
